Translation Samples

Chinese → English

English: The quantities on the two forms for each material need to correspond.

Focus: Workplace quality-control communication.

English → Chinese

English: I wanted to inform you before the night shift begins that the employee will not be able to report to work.

我想在夜班开始之前通知您，该员工无法来上班。

Focus: Professional workplace notification.

English → Chinese

English: The documents have already been printed. You took them earlier.

文件已经打印好了，您之前已经拿走了。

Focus: Concise workplace communication.

Interpretation Example

Chinese speaker: 今天下午把所有表格检查一遍，有问题的及时报告。

Interpreter: “Please check all the forms this afternoon and report any problems promptly.”

This demonstrates concise consecutive interpretation while preserving the speaker's intended meaning and professional tone.
